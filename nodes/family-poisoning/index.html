Poisoning, backdoors and persistence

Corrupt inputs to learning or persistent state to influence later behavior.

AI-generated draft · conceptual overview, not yet reviewed

This family groups techniques that corrupt the inputs to learning, or a system’s persistent state, so that influence planted earlier shapes behavior later. The shared mechanism is a delay between the tampering and its effect: an attacker contaminates data or embeds a hidden condition, and the payoff arrives when the model is trained, fine-tuned, or triggered downstream.

Members span the training lifecycle. Pretraining data poisoning and Instruction-tuning data poisoning contaminate corpora at different stages. Trigger-conditioned model backdoors and Persistent conditional LLM backdoors implant behavior that activates on a chosen trigger and may survive later safety training. Diffusion backdoors / BadDiffusion carries the same idea into image generation.

These route toward Establish persistent attacker influence, Manipulate outputs, predictions or decisions, and Violate intended behavioral constraints. The atlas marks trigger-conditioned backdoors as distinct from Unsafe checkpoint deserialization: both concern compromised artifacts, but a backdoor is learned behavior inside the weights, whereas unsafe loading is a code-execution flaw in how a checkpoint is read. Use this grouping to reason about persistence introduced through learning; the supply-chain delivery of tampered artifacts is catalogued separately.
